1. In the top bar next to the unit, choose a role, for example “view as treasurer”.
2. HUBIplan shows the menu, pages, buttons and also the help as someone with only this role sees them.
3. You can't change anything in this view.
4. With **End view** in the notice above the content you return.

You never see more than with your own rights.
