1. Open **My account** at the top right; you arrive at your [Profile](/mein-konto).
2. Choose the language under **Display language**. The page reloads straight away.

![The “Display language” choice in the profile](bild:konto-sprache)

The language only applies to you, including notification emails and the app. Receipts and letters
of the club stay in the club's language.
