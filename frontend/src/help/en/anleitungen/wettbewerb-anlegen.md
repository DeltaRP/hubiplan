1. Open [Competitions](/wettbewerbe) and click **New competition**.
2. Enter name and date and click **Create**.
3. Add the scorings: **Add individual ranking**, **Add team ranking** or a sub-unit scoring.
4. Record the results, at the range also on a second device via QR code.
5. Mark the ranking with **Mark as provisional** and decide ties in a shoot-off.
6. Finally click **Close as final** and print the certificates.
