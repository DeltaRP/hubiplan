1. Open **My account** at the top right and then [Sign-in & security](/mein-konto/anmeldung).
2. In the **Change password** section, enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter the **New password** twice (at least 8 characters, not too simple).
4. Click **Change password**.

![The “Change password” section under Sign-in & security](bild:konto-passwort)

Forgot your password? Sign out and choose “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page.
