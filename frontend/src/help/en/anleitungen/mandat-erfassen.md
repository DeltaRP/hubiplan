1. Get a signed SEPA mandate from the member (paper is enough).
2. Open [Finance › Direct debits](/finanzen/lastschrift) and there **SEPA mandates**.
3. Click **Record mandate**, choose the member and enter the account holder, IBAN and the date of signature.
4. Click **Save** and keep the paper.

If someone revokes the mandate, mark it as revoked there.
