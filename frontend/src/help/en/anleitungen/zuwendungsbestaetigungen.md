Before you start: the tax office, tax number and date of the notice are entered under the tax-exempt status details.

1. Open [Finance › Donation receipts](/finanzen/zuwendungen).
2. Record donations with **Record donation** or fetch them with **Take over from cash book**.
3. For a single donation, click **Issue receipt**.
4. At year end, click **Year end: confirm all**, check the list and issue them all.
