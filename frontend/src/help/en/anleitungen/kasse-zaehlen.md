1. Open [Finance › Cash count](/finanzen/kassenzaehlung) and click **Count cash** or, for a sales stand, **Festival cash report**.
2. Choose the cash box and enter notes and coins; HUBIplan calculates the total.
3. If there is a difference from the cash book, give the reason.
4. If a second person counts too, enter them (recommended).
5. Click **Save and book**.
